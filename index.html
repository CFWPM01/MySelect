Notes for developers

Scope

  • This is a static, front-end-only prototype. All data is mocked in-memory in the <script> block — nothing persists on reload.
  • The fixed prototype "today" is 7 Oct 2026; all login dates are generated relative to it.

MySelect Users (list)

  • Lists customers who have logged into MySelect, with last login and successful / unsuccessful login counts.
  • Clicking a row opens that customer's screen.

Customer screen

  • Shows name, email address (with a pencil to amend it), a "Resend login email" button, last login, and successful / unsuccessful login counts.
  • The email edit validates format and blocks an address already used by another MySelect customer. Saving updates the list view too.
  • "Resend login email" is simulated — it shows a confirmation and records when it was last sent.

Login Analytics

  • Overall MySelect logins, with a selector for: Daily MySelect account logins (all attempts), Successful logins, Unsuccessful logins.
  • Date range selector: last 7 / 14 / 30 days.
